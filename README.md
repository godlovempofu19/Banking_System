# Banking_System

ACETIC AXE BANK 

When you open the program, the first thing you see is the ACETIC AXE BANK name at the top, and then it takes you step by step through everything so you never get lost. It first asks how many users want to register, and it will only accept 3 to 10 people, then for each person it asks for their name, account number, PIN and starting balance in simple questions where you just type an answer and press Enter. After that it takes you to the login screen where you enter your account number and PIN, and if they are correct it welcomes you by name, but if they are wrong it tells you the details are wrong. Then the main menu appears with six clear numbered options for Deposit, Withdraw, Send Cash, Check Balance, Print Slip and Exit, and you just type the number for what you want to do.

Once you choose an option, the program asks you only what it needs, like how much you want to deposit or withdraw, or who you want to send cash to and how much, and it shows your new balance straight away and tells you in plain words if something is wrong, like not enough balance, the account does not exist, or you cannot send money to yourself. After every single transaction it always asks if you want a receipt, and if you choose Yes it prints a small slip with the bank name, your name, account number, the type of transaction, the amount and your new balance, and if you choose No it just goes back to the menu. The menu keeps showing again until you choose Exit, and then it shows the bank name one last time and says thank you for banking with ACETIC AXE BANK.
